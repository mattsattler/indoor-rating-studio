01 / WEIGHT DEVELOPMENT

Built for arena soccer.

The Northland Nomads rating framework starts from an independently chosen event-weight model. The original outdoor-style goal benchmark was +1.10; we selected +0.70 for this 5+GK format. That gives a 0.63636× multiplier (a 36.36% reduction), applied to most original event weights to preserve their relative priorities.

For V3 Beta, the shooting events were then adjusted separately: goals +0.70, saved/on-target shots +0.075, regular misses −0.025, blocked attempts 0, big misses −0.230, and exceptional misses −0.300. Position factors still affect each category; mixed-role performances use a playing-time-weighted average.

02 / SCORING FORMULA

How the score is calculated

6.5 + Σ (event count × event weight × position factor)

Points are summed for every recorded event, adjusted by the player's position (or weighted mix of positions), then limited to 3.0–10.0 and displayed to one decimal place. Minutes played are tracked but do not automatically normalize rating points. Score contributions can stack for distinct accomplishments, such as a completed pass that is also an assist. The same turnover or shot attempt must not be entered twice as different outcomes.

These position factors are design choices, not published MASL position ratings. The columns describe action types, not the five display categories: passes retain the original passing multiplier, dribbles the attacking multiplier, and ball-control errors the original discipline multiplier. For mixed roles, enter percentages totaling 100%; each event factor is a time-weighted average of the listed positions. Because individual events are not tagged by position, the blended factor is an estimate. A saved report retains its original weights; the event glossary below shows the current editable weights for new calculations.
